The thought of creating blogs hit me today morning and I thought it was the coolest idea ever. Having a place to dump my thoughts on various topics would be a very interesting place to visit in the future.

Sitting here and writing this very first entry though, I think it's going to be just as challenging as exciting.

I haven't done creative writing in quite a while, and this is honestly reminding me of college and high school days before I knew what LLM's even were. AI has definitely made us lazier, and my brain is not as nimble as I hoped it would be coming up with thoughts and converting them to words to type. Combating this is just another reason to write more frequently, and I aim to use MINIMAL AI (hopefully none at all, but it might get used to check grammar). I always like to set some goals too, so let write some for a year from now: 
1. Clearer thoughts in my head, and the ability to write/type them down being much easier
2. Having atleast 10 posts written

It would be a cool idea to also implement some sort of word counter, and keep track of which words I use frequently.

Anything I write will be with future me as the target audience, but if you're reading this, consider yourself invited to the conversation. If any of these ideas spark a thought you want to discuss, my inbox is always open 🤝.

Maybe I'll also make it a habit to end with a quote relating to whatever the entry is about.

Let me find one I heard a few months back on the relationship between writing and good thinking.

> "If you think you know something and don't write it down, you only *think* you know it"
